# Marketer offer revision 1

Status: proposed positioning implemented in local website; not customer-validated. Input authority is the user’s website brief, plus inspected repository capabilities. Company OS records are unavailable.

Promise: turn product knowledge into content you can review and publish. Audience hypothesis: founders and small marketing teams who know their product but need a repeatable production workflow. Buyer may also be the reviewer; an agency reviewer may have a different economic buyer. Desired result is a sustainable reviewed publishing routine, not a guaranteed quantity, ranking, revenue or headcount reduction.

Deliverables: short-form video drafts, article drafts, ad campaign drafts, review and scheduling workflows, saved content channels, library, supported automation interfaces. Prerequisites: accurate facts, audience and tone, permissions for supplied assets, appropriate account connections, generation credit, a person to review. Production latency depends on providers and settings; no twenty-minute or one-sentence guarantee.

Obstacles and treatment: blank-page uncertainty → public brief guide; scattered production → integrated workflow pages; output uncertainty → a small prepaid evaluation and explicit review; cost uncertainty → current catalog, usage explanation and future plan allowance; publication risk → review checklist and separate ad-spend disclosure; poor fit → each audience page states suitability and boundaries.

Current purchasable package: $5/$20/$50 USD prepaid credit, equal feature access. Proposed subscriptions: PRICING.md. Proposed channel counts and entitlement tiers are not currently enforced. Existing customers are not silently migrated. Do not call the proposal available or bind it to current Stripe packs.

No additional guarantee, inflated bonus stack, fake urgency, made-up customer logos, or discount anchors. Existing refund/legal pages retain their authority; copy links to them instead of expanding commitments. Email draft/contact requests are voluntary and no messages were sent during implementation.

Failure challenge: insufficient credit → inspect estimate before work; bad draft → revise/reject with costs clear; disconnected account → establish destination before planning publication; client isolation need → content channels explicitly are not tenants; needs guaranteed ROI → unsuitable promise, do not offer it. Source code does not establish end-to-end provider availability.

Research decision: observe how a prospective customer describes the job, which plan they can explain, whether the first draft was useful after review, what caused revisions, and whether they want a second publishing cycle. Only those observations can validate the proposed offer.
