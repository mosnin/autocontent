# Influence brief and proof registry

Version 1. Desired voluntary action: inspect a relevant workflow, understand costs, then create an account or prepare a demo request. Audience is a proposed segment, not an inferred vulnerability.

Mechanisms applied: reciprocity through complete ungated briefing/review/budget guides; commitment through evaluating one useful piece before expanding; authority through specific process explanations and candid limits; consistency through one shared pricing and FAQ source. No scarcity or unity framing is needed. No customer social proof is available, so the site contains no invented logos, testimonials, customer counts or Most popular badge. Graphics remain explicitly illustrative.

Proof registry: V1–V6 reference source-visible capabilities, not observed results; V7 is supplied artwork, not performance evidence; V8 is a priced proposal, not available subscriptions. Exact claims and their limits are in VALUE.md and source JSON. User may decline by reading more, choosing a prepaid pack, or leaving. No hidden enrollment, annual preselection, automatic extra spend, or email transmission.

Experiment: ask prospective users to explain what Marketer does, what they must provide, what a dollar of credit buys, whether proposed subscriptions can currently be purchased, and whether ad spend is included. Then observe the first reviewed production task. Repair the copy if misunderstanding survives. Measure qualified continuation and retention alongside cost surprise, refund requests and review effort; do not optimize clicks in isolation.

Distribution: implement on the existing website only. No outbound messages, paid campaigns or production release executed. All work is a local, reviewable draft.
