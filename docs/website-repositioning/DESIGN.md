## Evidence and decisions

Repository inspected: homepage, navigation, feature and solution pages, billing/packs.py, products.ts, contact form, FAQ and sitemap. Source evidence describes implemented capabilities; it is not a live provider trial or customer outcome. Company OS and Symbolic tools were searched and unavailable. Pricing, Value, Influence context gates remain missing-integration. Strategy is a proposed positioning grounded in inspected source and user intent, not validated customer research.

Primary audience hypothesis: founders and small marketing teams with product expertise and recurring content work. Secondary: creators, ecommerce, agencies, local businesses and developers operating agents. No revenue, time-saved, conversion or ranking guarantees. Preserve the platform scope including video, search content and paid campaign drafts.

Critical journey: understand outcome → inspect appropriate product/solution → understand process and prerequisites → compare prepaid credits → create account or request a demo. Preserve app and auth; inspect links into them without pretending to run a paid generation.

Baseline findings: headline sells an agent rather than a result; inner pages explain implementation internals; homepage $5 video estimates contradict pricing page; pay-for-shipping copy contradicts usage billing; Most popular has no adoption evidence; generic social platform links imply nonexistent company profiles; Solutions is hidden in Resources. Existing animation, campaign assets, palette, typography, pill controls and page rhythm are the visual foundation.

## Visual plan resolved before implementation

Hierarchy: first an exact outcome headline, second a concrete mechanism and customer responsibility, third Get started and How it works. Product pages lead with the job, then an existing campaign image or product illustration, problem/benefit sections, workflow, fit/limits and relevant next steps. Quiet pricing disclosures stay readable beside commitments.

Composition: preserve homepage orbit, film, scrolling manifesto, feature rows, sticky product walkthrough, gallery, integration art and inverted final CTA. Expand inner pages with existing FeatureHero, SectionHeading, Reveal and SectionCta primitives. Preserve 1440px container and 20/32/40px responsive insets. Product, Solutions, Resources, Company, Pricing navigation order. Footer has Use cases, Product, Company, Resources, Legal.

Material: inherited warm light/dark surfaces, tokens, rounding and supplied artwork. No new design system or stock graphics. User-requested purchased graphics take precedence over generic skill defaults against decoration. No logos used as customer proof without evidence; campaign images are explicitly identified as illustrative artwork.

Details: inherited font roles and medium display weight; headline fitting checked at 390, 768 and 1440 widths. Meaningful links, no fake popular badge, complete currency/usage terms, consistent Get started labels. Existing motion primitives and reduced-motion alternatives. Avoid new changing-number or haptic controls because there is no corresponding task.

Responsive: hero CTAs wrap/stack at narrow width, feature rows and reading sections become one column; no fixed copy heights. Desktop menus remain within viewport; mobile disclosures cover identical categories. Focus, Escape dismissal and current-page state checked.

Alternative considered: an all-in-one autonomous marketing-department promise. Rejected because it implies strategy, demand and unattended delivery beyond source evidence. Selected direction retains the visual treatment with specific production outcomes. Self-review, no independent reviewer or representative user evidence.
